## Relic category

Heart of the Sun, all three Full Moons, Fleeting Illusion, Deadlock and Cursed Moon are Relics. They are held rather than played and do not count as Legendaries. Winning priorities remain unchanged. Cursed Moon is a penalty Relic, not a Full Moon.

# Heart of Hearts — Current Rules

- Cursed Moon is only a penalty, not a Full Moon. Fleeting Illusion only replaces the Sun. Deadlock wins an ending tie.
- Lockdown lasts through the next four scheduled turns, including its owner’s next turn; skipped turns count. It blocks Legendary reactions too.
- Heartbreak blocks playing Heart-suit cards, including both revival cards, but allows receiving healing.
- Reset includes hand cards, face-up effects and a protected card. Stop drawing immediately after the first Instant is drawn and dealt with.
- Mimic copies the exact Utility, Heart, Legendary or other Joker on top of the discard pile. No Instants or circular copies. Masquerade chooses a Legendary of the same J/Q/K/A rank. Both obey copied costs and reaction timing. Ongoing copies retain their Joker identity.
- Legendary count includes J/Q/K/A and Jokers. Special/Win Condition cards, Cursed Moon and Dud do not count.
- A purple back does not make a card Legendary. Legendary interactions use J/Q/K/A and Jokers only.
- Two of six Jokers are included. Eight Instants are active, including Exposed Heart.
- Choose a Legendary’s player target before its universal three-second reaction window, even with no eligible responses. Return-card reactions use ten seconds.
- Rewind restores physical game state at the start of your previous turn, then removes itself. Information already seen cannot be forgotten.
- Only the top discard is visible. Recovery may privately show eligible discards when its effect resolves.
- Returning hand cards shuffles Legendaries only. Utility and Heart cards are discarded. Last Resort explicitly returns random discards.
- Removing a face-up effect by paying its cost uses 1 action. Destroying active effects with The Magician also uses 1 action.
- If both revival cards are held, choose one to use. Heartbreak prevents both from triggering.
- Rabbit Hole uses the former Q♥ search: lose 1 Heart, inspect cards sequentially, optionally take one, then shuffle all remaining cards.
- Pending rule: eighth Instant replacement. Special cards retain their existing return-to-deck behavior on death as an explicit provisional rule, not Legendary classification.

## Crocodile Tears 
At exactly 1 Heart, automatically trigger after another player’s directly targeted card resolves against you. They cannot play Legendaries for the rest of the game. Remove this card. Global effects do not trigger it.

## Mimic 
Copy the exact Utility, Heart, Legendary or other Joker on top of the discard pile. Cannot copy Instants or non-playable cards. Follow its costs, conditions and reaction timing. Circular copying is not allowed. Return to Mimic after resolving, unless the copied effect moves or removes it. Ongoing Puppet Master and Guardian Angel copies keep their appearance with a Joker badge until the effect ends.

## Rabbit Hole 
Lose 1 Heart. Secretly search the Draw Pile one card at a time. Take the current card to stop, or skip it and continue. You cannot return to skipped cards. Skipped cards stay in the deck. Shuffle the remaining deck after the search.

## Exposed Heart — Instant (×1)

When drawn, lose 1 Heart, then briefly reveal every Legendary in your hand to all players. Return those cards to your hand, then discard this Instant.
