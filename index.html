     HEART OF HEARTS

    How to play

    🏆 Win Conditions

    1 ☀️ + 🌕 · Heart of the Sun + Full Moon

    The primary win. Puppet Master redirects this win to its living original caster.

    2 🎭 + 🌕 · Fleeting Illusion + Full Moon

    Only if nobody achieves the primary win, Fleeting Illusion replaces the Sun. Puppet Master redirects this win too.

    3 🃏 · Most Legendaries

    Count J/Q/K/A and Jokers, including protected cards. Relics and Dud do not count.

    4 🔐 · Deadlock

    If the result is tied, the living Deadlock holder wins, even outside the tied players. Without Deadlock, tied winners share the win.

    Start with 3 Hearts and 5 private cards. A random player begins.

    Eight normal Instants are available. Heart Collector and Death’s Door are guaranteed and cannot be replaced. They enter after dealing.

    ✦ Relics

    Relics are their own category: Heart of the Sun, Full Moon (3 copies), Fleeting Illusion, Deadlock and Cursed Moon. Keep them in your hand; they are not played and do not count as Legendaries. Sun + Full Moon is the primary win; Fleeting Illusion + Full Moon is the fallback win; Deadlock decides a tie. Cursed Moon is a penalty Relic: lose 1 Heart at game end, then discard it. It does not count as a Full Moon.

    🎴 Your turn

    Paying to remove a face-up effect costs 1 action, regardless of the number of cards paid.

    Use up to 3 actions: play Utility / Heart cards or remove a face-up effect by paying its removal cost. Then play 1 Legendary or draw to end your turn. Encore allows 2 Legendaries on your next turn. Foresight ends your turn without drawing.

    Drawn Instants appear in the middle before resolving. Altered Fate returns yours to the top and ends your turn. Nullifier destroys it. Sacrifice must be removed before playing a Legendary.

    Choose the player target first, then every Legendary play has a universal 3-second reaction window. Any player may react. Return-card reactions allow 10 seconds. Private decision prompts allow 30 seconds.

    🃏 Jokers & purple backs

    Only 2 of 6 Jokers enter each game. Pandora can privately exchange with the hidden excluded pool. Mimic copies the exact top discard, including other Jokers but not Instants. Masquerade chooses any Legendary matching the top J/Q/K/A rank.

    Purple backs do not guarantee a scoring Legendary. Only cards classified as Legendary, including Jokers, qualify for Legendary interactions. Sun, Full Moons, Fleeting Illusion, Deadlock, Cursed Moon, Dud and Instants also have purple backs.

    ❤️ Hearts & ending effects

    The last completed draw ends the game if the deck remains empty. Parasite kills its holder; Cursed and Cursed Moon each cost 1 Heart. Discard Cursed Moon afterward. 2♥ Final Blessing revives you with full Hearts; 3♥ Second Chance revives you with 1 Heart. If you hold both, choose one.

    Heartbreak blocks Heart-suit plays, including both revival cards, but allows receiving healing. Death returns Legendaries to the shuffled deck and discards Basic hand cards.
